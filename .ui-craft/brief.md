# samu.quest design brief

## Purpose and audience
Help hiring teams and clients evaluate Samuel Barnes for growth, marketing, community leadership, and agentic engineering. Professional work and personal projects remain separate.

## Binding preferences
Keep the approved Tektur and Bricolage Grotesque pairing, dark default with light mode, a restrained spectrum with personal pink, exact hero subheading, verified family photograph, CV, UN talk, and contact paths. Use authentic project logos rather than homepage previews. Preserve factual responsibilities and team attribution.

## Learned constraints
- October 8: the signal-path S logo was rejected. Use a recognizable Samuel Barnes monogram; initials must read at navigation size.
- October 8: the chrome/mycelium hero was rejected. Show actual work through an interactive project map; avoid generic AI sculpture or stock imagery.
- October 8: company logos and project sections need fuller context and consistent optical weight.
- Previous passes: recruiter-friendly copy; no mystical metaphors, inflated aptitude claims, or invented results.

## Current pass
Reskin; preserve section order and URLs. Signature: a linked work map, built from authentic project marks and four accessible links. Every node remains legible without hover or JavaScript. Keep motion tied to arrival, focus, pointer interaction, and scrolling, with reduced-motion support.

## Voice
Direct, knowledgeable, approachable. Short factual sentences. Each project explains purpose and useful capabilities. Do not infer unconfirmed personal roles.

- October 8, second pass: lime was too pervasive. Use peach for actions/contact, blue for engineering, mint for applied systems, and pink for community/personal work. Preserve the dark foundation and clear professional voice. Add tactile texture and expressive but responsive motion.
