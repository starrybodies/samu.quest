The House of Reverence and the Ridiculous

Ceremony-grade gatherings. Dancefloor first.

Sacred/Saucy launched in 2026 as the production arm of Salt Spring Island’s music, festival, nightlife, and ritual culture. The duality is the point: deep reverence for collective prayer ceremony, fused with next-level global low-end soundsystem and dance culture.

The Island has long been a beacon for the seekers and mycophiles, the dreamers and the yesters, the phrocks and the freaks. We are its Keeper of flow — the bridge between legacy and luminess, nurturing the qualities that make this place timeless.
